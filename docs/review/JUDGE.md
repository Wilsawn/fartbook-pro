**Likely award: Stupider.** Polished and funny, but fart instruments are expected here, and polish reads "nice music toy," not "why does this exist." Stupidest needs the uncle bit and the hand-off to both kill.

## First 5 seconds
Hook: silent tap, laptop farts, "That was the laptop." Lose me if row 3 can't see the tap or the first hit is silent (venue noise, Safari audio asleep). The WET visual is small; sound carries it.

## The moment I'd remember
"uncle..." then "not now.", the cushion's deadpan side-eye (`s06`), full-screen green fart (`s08`). But the burst lasts under 0.5 s (0.43 s sample).

## What loses to a competitor
A more original premise with a physical prop. Also: "Socratica did MacBook sensors," and the biggest laugh is borrowed YouTube audio.

## Bugs found in the run
- Loop playback adds stink. **EVACUATE BAYVIEW YARDS plus screen shake hits about 20 s in** and stays 30 s (`s12`), so the 52 s finale has nowhere to climb.
- No "I'm sorry" caption; a squiggle balloon covers the cushion all second half.
- No product shot: at 58 s the loop runs and stink is back to "concerning" (`s23`).
- PITCH.md says press `[` before the hand-off. `[` *lowers* the threshold (more misfires). The hand-off mute is **M**.

## 5 changes, ranked
1. **Stink only from live hits (or capped at 75 until uncle fart 3).** EVACUATE lands as the climax; no 30 s shake.
2. **Make the tap visible:** phone cam on the hands, or a ripple from the edge tapped plus a live sensor trace. Surprise goes from "buttons" to "wait, how?"
3. **End card:** stop the loop, clean cushion product shot plus tagline (or the fart receipt). Clean applause beat.
4. **Shrink the hand-off to a judge-triggered final fart, and fix `[` to M.** Cuts the riskiest 12 s; laughs get room.
5. **Caption "I'm sorry," drop the persistent balloon, use a longer whinier uncle fart.** Gives 15 to 34 s a second laugh.

## Tough questions
1. **"Socratica showed MacBook sensors already. What's new?"** Win: "They proved the sensors. I turned one accelerometer into a whole-chassis drum kit: 8 taps per spot, nearest-neighbour on push and twist, [X]% on this laptop today. Unsure taps are silent."
2. **"Hands off the keyboard. Tap where I point."** Win: do it, the right fart plays. Or let the judge tap.
3. **"What was built today, what did AI write, and do you have rights to the uncle clip?"** Win: a plain split matching Devpost, "Claude Code pair-programmed, I calibrated and tuned here," "permission requested, not in the public repo."
