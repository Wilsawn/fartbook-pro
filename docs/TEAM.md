# FartBook team brief (every agent reads this first, and appends to the log at the bottom)

## The product
A MacBook you play like a fart instrument at Stupid Ideas Hackathon Ottawa (today, demo 5:30 PM,
60 seconds on stage). Silver sides = drum squares (motion sensor), lid = bellows filling an air
tank, trackpad = uncle vocal chops + air-powered fart notes, everything loops. Song: "Uncle, Not
Now" remix (docs/DEMO.md). Read README.md and CLAUDE.md for architecture.

## The owner (Delo), what he wants
- Stupid but genuinely funny and delightful. References he loves: **Patatap** (press a key, bold
  sound + bold animated shape) and **Chrome Music Lab** (toy-like, anyone can play instantly).
- NO AI slop: no generic gradients-and-glow SaaS look, no emoji as graphics, no template chrome,
  no cards-for-everything. Every visual choice should be specific to farts / whoopee cushions.
- High quality, works on stage first try, fast to set up. Honest about what is untested.
- Plain words in any on-screen text. Never use em dashes.

## Hook API (web/app.js owns sound + logic; other modules only listen)
`window.FB.bus` is an EventTarget. Events (`e.detail`):
- `audio-ready` {master (Tone.Limiter), context (AudioContext)}
- `hit` {kind: 'drum'|'pad'|'note', name, vel 0..1, el (the square/pad element), group: 'drum'|'vocal'|'uncle'|'note'}
- `frame` every animation frame {air 0..100, stink 0..100, loopPos 0..1, looping, recording, events[{step,name,kind}], steps}
- `freshener` {} and `outofair` {note}
Set `FB.customVisuals = true` to take over hit visuals (app.js then only toggles the `.hit` class).
`FB.state`, `FB.master`, `FB.ZONE_SOUND` are readable. Do not change app.js logic; if you need a
new event, write it in the log as a request for the integrator.

## How to test (sandbox)
`python3 src/sensor_server.py --mock &` and `python3 src/bridge.py &` (port 8765; if busy:
`pkill -f bridge.py; pkill -f sensor_server.py` first). Playwright works: `require('playwright')`,
`chromium.launch({args:['--autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required']})`, open http://127.0.0.1:8765,
click #startBtn, press keys (A Z K , 1 8 Q G SPACE), screenshot at 1440x900, check the console is
clean. Look at your screenshots before you call it done.

## Review board (runs after the departments)
1. **Delo** persona: is it funny, is it Patatap-level delightful, any slop, would I be proud on stage.
2. **Senior engineer:** bugs, made-up APIs, demo-breaking risks.
3. **Hackathon judge:** in 60 seconds, would this win "stupidest idea, best execution".
A chief reviewer merges their verdicts into one ranked fix list.
