# 60-second demo: "Uncle, Not Now (FartBook Remix)"

Setup: `./scripts/start.sh` (one command: bridge, trackpad reader, sensors, opens Safari). Safari full screen
(Cmd+Ctrl+F), volume 80%, calibrated at the venue, loop cleared, trackpad pill says "trackpad live", uncle bank
showing, 3/4-finger trackpad gestures turned off (System Settings > Trackpad > More Gestures).

Trackpad map (touch, no clicking). Uncle bank: top row uncle, not now, chop 3, chop 4; bottom row fart 1, fart 2,
fart 3, long fart. Tab = notes bank: bottom row A C D E (low), top row G A C D (high). Notes need air: pump the lid.

1. **0 to 8 s, hook.** (Say nothing, tap the left side, let it fart.) "That was the laptop. Apple hid a motion sensor in this MacBook and never told anyone. I used it for farts."
2. **8 to 15 s, the meme.** Trackpad top left then next square ("Uncle... not now"), pause, bottom left (fart 1). Wait for the laugh.
3. **15 to 28 s, the beat.** SPACE, tap KICK / SNARE on the silver sides. It loops. Add SQUEAK hats.
4. **28 to 40 s, chorus.** Stutter the "I'm sorry" chop on the beat (choke group keeps it clean).
5. **40 to 52 s, bridge.** Pump the lid (the cushion puffs up), Tab to notes, play a sad fart melody on the trackpad (bottom row low, top row high). Tab back.
6. **52 to 60 s, drop.** Trackpad fart 3 or long fart (the cushion's lips spray), Stink-O-Meter hits EVACUATE, cover the camera: air freshener. "FartBook Pro. Your laptop was a whoopee cushion the whole time."

Judge moment: hand the laptop over: "pump the lid, now touch a square." (Press M first so their grip does not fire the side drums.)

Fallbacks: sensors fail, play everything from the keyboard (A Z K , drums; 1 2 3 4 vocals; 8 9 0 H farts; Q to I notes). Trackpad reader fails: Ctrl+C it and just click the squares. App crashes, show the backup video, restart.
